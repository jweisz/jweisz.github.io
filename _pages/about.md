I'm a research scientist in **human-centered AI**. I study how to design AI systems that amplify and augment human capabilities. My work addresses three broad areas:

- **Useful AI**: How do we build AI that enhances people's productivity and fits into their workflows, in areas such as software engineering, design, and product management?
- **Safe AI**: How do we help the people who build and use AI understand its risks and choose the right mitigations?
- **Personal AI**: How can AI systems model their users' goals, knowledge, and intentions, and how can they make those models visible so that people and AI develop a _mutual_ understanding of each other?

I use a mixed-methods approach in my research:

- **qualitative** studies to understand a problem space,
- **prototyping** to explore new forms of interaction,
- **controlled** experiments to measure effects,
- **crowdsourcing** to gather feedback at scale, and
- **speculative design** to reason about new research directions and longer-term consequences of technology.

One lesson runs through much of my work: [AI doesn't have to be perfect to be useful](/research/#ai-for-software-engineering), but you can't just hand people AI and expect them to become more productive. To be useful, AI has to fit into people's workflows, minimize harm, and adapt to the people using it. Check out my [research](/research/) for more detail.

For over fifteen years, I worked at **IBM Research** where I co-led our global research strategy on Human-Centered Trustworthy AI and managed the Human-AI Collaboration team. A few of my major contributions at IBM include:

- the development of a set of [Design Principles for Generative AI Applications](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3613904.3642466) and design thinking exercises, which trained and enabled 3,000+ design practitioners at IBM to incorporate AI as a design material
- the first version of the [IBM AI Risk Atlas](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/watsonx/saas?topic=ai-risk-atlas)
- conversational programming assistants, such as the [Programmer's Assistant](/research/#ai-for-software-engineering) and the [Agentic Code Explorer](/research/#personalized-code-explanations), which informed the design of [watsonx Code Assistant](https://cloud.ibm.com/docs/watsonx-code-assistant)
- open source projects such as [TJBot](/projects/tjbot/) and [Entanglion](/projects/entanglion/) that have educated hundreds of thousands of students and makers about AI and quantum computing

I've co-organized long-running workshop series on human-AI co-creation ([HAI-GEN](https://hai-gen.github.io/2025)), human-centered explainable AI ([HCXAI](https://hcxai.jimdosite.com)), and theory of mind in human-AI interaction ([ToMinHAI](https://tominhai-cui2025.github.io)). I also founded the [Human-Centered AI](https://medium.com/human-centered-ai) publication on Medium. I was named an IBM Master Inventor and hold 35+ US patents.

Today, I advise startups on AI research and product strategy. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/).
