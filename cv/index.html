Professional Summary

Research leader in human-centered AI focused on designing useful, safe, and personal AI systems that amplify and augment human capabilities. ACM Senior Member with numerous publications in top-tier HCI & AI venues including CHI, IUI, CSCW, FAccT, AIES, ICML, and IJCAI. Research topics span human-AI collaboration & co-creativity; agentic AI; responsible & human-centered explainable AI (HCXAI); and AI risk, governance, and evaluation. Versed in qualitative, quantitative, prototyping, crowdsourcing, and speculative design methods, with extensive experience collaborating with AI & ML researchers, engineers, product managers, and designers to prototype the future and deliver research insights into shipped products.
