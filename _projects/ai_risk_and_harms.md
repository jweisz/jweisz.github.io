Generative and agentic AI systems create new kinds of risk. My work in this area identifies those risks and helps both developers and users recognize and mitigate them.

## Mapping the risks of AI

The guidance we wrote on the risks of generative AI was adopted by IBM's AI Ethics Board and became the first version of the [IBM AI Risk Atlas](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/watsonx/saas?topic=ai-risk-atlas). More recently, we interviewed people who build AI agents about how they perceive and prioritize the risks of agentic AI. I also co-organized the AgentCraft workshop on developing trustworthy agentic AI systems at IUI 2026.

## Factuality and transparency

LLMs can produce output that sounds confident but is wrong. We studied how visual factuality indicators and source attributions help people judge the reliability of LLM output.

{% cite DoOGMWW25 abs-2405-20434 %}

## Contextual privacy

People can overshare sensitive information with conversational agents, and agents can pass that information along in ways people don't expect. We developed methods that protect people from oversharing with conversational agents, and a way to evaluate contextual privacy across agentic workflows.
