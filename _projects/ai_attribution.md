Who deserves credit when people create with generative AI? And how should they disclose that AI was involved?

## Which contributions deserve credit?

In a scenario-based study, we found that people feel AI deserves credit for its contributions, especially when it synthesizes information, contributes new ideas, or produces large amounts of material. _How_ AI was used matters, not only _whether_ it was used. Many organizational AI-use policies, however, only require disclosing use.

## The AI Attribution Toolkit

We built the [AI Attribution Toolkit](https://aiattribution.github.io/) to give creators a more precise language for describing how AI contributed to their work. Creators answer a few questions about how they used AI, and the toolkit produces an attribution statement they can include with their work.

## Overcoming barriers to disclosure

People often hesitate to disclose AI use because they worry about how others will perceive them. We examined how the granularity of a disclosure, along with sociocultural enablers, affects the stigma people perceive around disclosing AI use.
