Many human-AI teaming studies show that AI doesn't always deliver the performance gains we expect. We hypothesized that teams would perform better if the human and the AI held a **mutual theory of mind**: an accurate model of each other's goals, knowledge, and reasoning.

The cooperative party game _Wavelength_ rewards teammates who understand each other well, which makes it a good testbed for this question. We built a version where people play alongside an LLM-based agent. We then tested two interventions: introductions with targeted questions before play, and retrospectives where players share their strategies and the reasoning behind their clues. Teams that had these interventions outperformed teams that did not.
