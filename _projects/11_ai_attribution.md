Who deserves credit when people create with generative AI? In a scenario-based study, we found that people feel AI deserves credit for its contributions, especially when it synthesizes information, contributes new ideas, or produces large amounts of material. _How_ AI was used matters, not only _whether_ it was used. Many organizational AI-use policies, however, only require disclosing use.

We built the [AI Attribution Toolkit](https://aiattribution.github.io/) to give creators a more precise language for describing how AI contributed to their work. Follow-up work examines how the granularity of a disclosure, along with sociocultural factors, affects the stigma people perceive around disclosing AI use.
