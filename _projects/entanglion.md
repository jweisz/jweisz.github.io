[Entanglion](https://entanglion.github.io) is a cooperative board game for two players, designed at IBM Research. It introduces players to the fundamental concepts of quantum computing: qubits, superposition, entanglement, measurement, and error. It also introduces the different hardware and software components involved in building a real quantum computer.

Players work together to navigate the three galaxies of the quantum universe, avoid detection by the defense mechanisms left behind by an ancient race, and rebuild a quantum computer. Entanglion has been **used in hundreds of high school and university classrooms** as part of their curriculum.
