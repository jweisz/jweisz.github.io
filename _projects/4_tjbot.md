[TJBot](https://ibmtjbot.github.io) is an open source, DIY cardboard robot that we created at IBM Research to make it easy and fun to learn how to program with AI. Makers can laser cut or 3D print TJBot's body, add a Raspberry Pi and a few inexpensive components, and then write "recipes" that let TJBot listen, speak, see, shine its LED, and wave its arm using IBM Watson AI services.

TJBot has been **used by hundreds of thousands of students and makers** around the world. I was also recognized with an IBM Volunteer Excellence Award for TJBot classroom learning activities supporting Vietnam Teacher's Day.

The original software is in [ibmtjbot/tjbot](https://github.com/ibmtjbot/tjbot) and [node-tjbotlib](https://github.com/ibmtjbot/node-tjbotlib). Active development has moved to [TJBot Community Edition](/projects/1_tjbot_ce/). Other things I built for TJBot include the [TJBot Swift Playground](https://github.com/jweisz/tjbot-playground), [swifty-tj](https://github.com/jweisz/swifty-tj), and [tjbot-daemon](https://github.com/jweisz/tjbot-daemon).
